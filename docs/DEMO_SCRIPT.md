# Demo script

## Team-formation pitch (45 seconds, 10:00)

"Every library has a shelf of books people start and never finish. Nobody tells you
what to read first. PreReads: scan a book, get a reading line — the two or three books
that prepare you, the book, and where each one is on a shelf today, by branch. The
engine is running. I need a designer for the line and the card, and a reader who can
tell me when a route is wrong. Who's in?"

## Finalist pitch (2 minutes, 16:30)

1. The problem in one line (the most-abandoned-books list).
2. Live: a judge names a book; type it; the line appears; tap a stop; read the why.
3. How it works in 20 seconds: propose → verify in the catalogue → score readiness →
   judge pairs → rules in code → join availability by branch. "Remove the engine and
   there is no product."
4. Who it's for: the reader at the shelf, the senior who needs plain text, the librarian
   who can reorder a line.
5. What's next: branch reading identity from checkout trends, Meet-at-the-book for
   book clubs, printable shelf-talkers.

## Open-mic ask (15:30)

"Which book have you been avoiding? Come to our table, scan it, leave with the line."
